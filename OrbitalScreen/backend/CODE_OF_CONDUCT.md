# Code of Conduct

## Welcome
Welcome to an environment created to assist the masses, in an effort to help many.  
OrbitalScreen is built to empower everyone — from enterprise and industry to small businesses, households, and creators.

## Our Pledge
We pledge to maintain an inclusive, harassment‑free, and respectful environment for all participants.

## Our Standards

| Behavior Type | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| 🟢 **Positive** | Inclusive language, respect for others, constructive feedback, collaboration |
| 🟡 **Caution** | Off-topic banter, excessive self-promotion, minor disruption |
| 🔴 **Unacceptable** | Harassment, trolling, private info leaks, discrimination, sustained disruption |

> 🛡️ Maintainers may act swiftly to protect the community from red‑level behavior. Yellow behavior may receive a warning or moderation.

## Our Constant Pitch
**OrbitalScreen — The Future, Made Present. The Future, Under Control. Empowering Everyone.**  
This community exists so others can share a little part of the future with us.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, issues, pull requests, and community interactions.

## Enforcement

**Escalation Path:**
1. 🟡 **Warning** — for minor or first‑time issues  
2. 🟠 **Temporary Restrictions** — for repeat or serious violations  
3. 🔴 **Permanent Removal** — for sustained harassment or disruption

**Final Decision Authority:**  
Maintainers have full authority to enforce this Code of Conduct.  
This doctrine ensures OrbitalScreen remains compliance‑ready, audit‑ready, and trusted as a service node.
